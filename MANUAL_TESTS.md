# Manual Test Checklist

1. Open the app and confirm the page loads with the title "Little Moments".
2. Check the layout on a narrow screen width and confirm the form stacks into one column.
3. Click "Find an activity" with default filters and confirm an activity card appears.
4. Pick `Outdoors` and `Up to 5 minutes`, then confirm either a matching activity appears or the empty-state message explains there is no exact match.
5. Click "Surprise me" several times and confirm different activities can appear.
6. Confirm every shown activity includes setup, steps, and safety notes.
7. Confirm the page does not ask for names, birthdays, photos, or contact details.
8. Refresh the page and confirm no previous activity selection is required for the app to work.
